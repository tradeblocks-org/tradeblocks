# TradeBlocks 4.4.0

**Is this filter real, or just the best threshold you happened to try?**

4.4 adds rank correlation to predictive-field discovery and opt-in selection-adjusted statistics to `filter_curve`. Compare multiple fields, inspect a chosen threshold year by year, and test its best observed result against shuffled calendar-month outcome blocks. These are exploratory diagnostics with explicit assumptions and work limits, not proof of a profitable filter or an out-of-sample validation. Existing calls without the new options retain their defaults and response shapes.

## Behavior changes

### Existing calls stay unchanged; new options add analysis

`find_predictive_fields` still defaults to Pearson. Omitting `method` retains its existing response shape; an explicit method is reported in the result.

A single-field `filter_curve` call without `fields`, `objective` or `permutation` retains its existing response. Any of those new options adds best-threshold and per-year analysis. Multi-field calls return per-field results under `fields`, including named unavailable/uncovered entries. Existing covered/uncovered baselines, causal dataset availability and `maxMissedSessions` remain in force. The legacy `sweetSpots` criteria do not change.

The new `filter_curve` objective selects the best eligible candidate **even when its improvement is negative**. This differs from `suggest_filters` admission: a fitted optimum is not automatically a recommendation. Work-limit refusals are explicit rather than silently reducing iterations, dropping requested fields or changing the method.

## Predictive rank correlation

`find_predictive_fields` accepts `method: "pearson" | "spearman" | "kendall"`:

- **Pearson** remains the default linear correlation.
- **Spearman** uses ranks to describe monotone relationships, including nonlinear ones.
- **Kendall** uses tie-adjusted Kendall tau-b.

Ranking uses the selected coefficient on the same covered field/target pairs, after strategy/date filtering and causal entry-time resolution. Dataset freshness refusals still honor `maxMissedSessions`. Selecting a rank coefficient does not establish causation, profitability or stability outside the sample.

### Kendall work limit

Kendall is quadratic. The shared ranking module admits at most **75 million estimated pair comparisons across fields**, summing `n × (n - 1) / 2` for each field's valid field/target coverage and counting only fields meeting `minSamples`. It checks the budget before quadratic field calculations. A 1,100-trade block with 100 fully covered fields uses 60,445,000 pairs; a single fully covered 20,000-trade field exceeds the limit.

The public library exports `KendallPairWorkLimitError` with estimated-pair, field-count, trade-count and limit metadata. MCP reports `status: "refused"` and `reason: "kendall_pair_work_limit"` with that budget metadata. Pearson and Spearman do not acquire this pair-work cap. Library callers can also select the method through `rankPredictiveTradeFields`; `spearmanCorrelation` becomes a public export.

## Multi-field filter curves and objectives

Supply exactly one of `field: "signal.score"` or `fields: ["signal.score", "other.score"]`, with at most **10 distinct fields**. Each field uses its own covered population and reports unavailable values explicitly.

`objective` accepts `totalPl` or `winRate`; opting into multi-field or permutation analysis defaults it to `totalPl`. Candidates must keep and remove at least one covered trade. Comparisons are strict: values equal to a threshold are removed from the selected `<` or `>` cohort.

- `totalPl` ranks total P&L improvement first, with win-rate gain breaking ties.
- `winRate` ranks win-rate gain in percentage points first, with P&L improvement breaking ties.

`bestThreshold` reports the selected threshold/direction, kept and removed populations and objective delta. `mode` continues to select `lt`, `gt` or `both`; selection searches the requested directions and thresholds. The same ranking rule applies to observed and permuted outcomes.

### Per-year stability

`perYear` applies the **one full-sample chosen threshold**, without re-fitting it separately each year. Rows partition trades by entry calendar year and show covered baseline, kept/removed counts, P&L, win rate and objective delta, plus uncovered count/P&L. Uncovered-only years remain visible. Fewer than 30 baseline, kept or removed entries adds a low-sample warning rather than dropping the year. This is a descriptive stability check, not a walk-forward test.

## Selection-adjusted calendar-month permutation

For example:

```json
{
  "blockId": "sample",
  "fields": ["custom.signal", "custom.noise"],
  "objective": "totalPl",
  "permutation": { "iterations": 499, "block": "month", "seed": 42 }
}
```

Pass those arguments to `filter_curve`. Permutation is opt-in; `block` is `month`, the seed defaults to 42 and iterations must be an integer from 1 to **499**.

Covered trades are stably ordered by entry date and their P&L outcomes grouped into observed calendar-month blocks. Each draw shuffles whole blocks, retaining their internal outcome order, then pairs the flattened outcomes with the fixed field series. It reruns the full requested threshold/direction sweep and compares the permuted best objective delta with the observed best, adjusting for the threshold search within that field rather than testing only the already-selected winner.

**Assumptions and limits:** month blocks preserve dependence within a month but **not across months**. Unequal month sizes are retained; the method assumes exchangeable monthly outcome blocks. That assumption is not established by receiving a small p-value. The seeded generator makes identical requests deterministic, not independently validated.

`pValue` is the add-one estimate `(exceedances + 1) / (iterations + 1)`, counting ties as exceedances. At least **30 covered trades and 8 observed months** are required. Named refusal reasons include `too_few_covered_trades`, `too_few_blocks` and `no_eligible_threshold`; refused tests receive no fabricated p-value.

### Holm adjustment covers one requested family

`pValueHolm` applies Holm adjustment across **all fields requested in this one call**. Refused/unavailable fields conservatively occupy p=1 in the family calculation, without being given invented p-values in their own output. With one field there is no across-field adjustment.

This does not account for field, objective or mode choices across separate calls, repeated exploration, or out-of-sample performance. A succession of requests is not one automatically tracked testing family. Threshold-selection adjustment and within-request Holm correction do not make the result a causal or live-trading claim.

### Permutation work limits

Only requests with `permutation` limit `thresholds` and `percentileSteps` to **100 entries each**. Exceeding either returns `permutation_candidate_limit` before any sweep. Larger arrays without permutation retain their existing schema acceptance; 100 is not a new general threshold limit.

Permutation work is admitted before any field sweep, using each field's covered trade count `n`, requested candidate count `t` before deduplication, and iterations `r`:

`(r + 2) × (n × ceil(log2(max(2,n))) + 6nt + 3t × ceil(log2(max(2,2t))))`

The estimate includes repeated sorting and scoring, with caps of **30 million work units per field** and **90 million per request**, summed across requested fields. At **1,100 covered trades, 499 iterations and the seven default percentile thresholds**, each fully covered field costs 29,250,384 units. **Three fields** fit (87,751,152 units); four do not (117,001,536). A 55-observed-month dataset at that size also meets the minimum-month requirement, but the work estimate itself does not use month count.

Exceeding either budget refuses the **whole request**, not just one field, with `permutation_work_limit` or `permutation_request_work_limit`, estimates and limits. These are computational caps, not a wall-clock guarantee; threshold deduplication does not expand the allowance.
