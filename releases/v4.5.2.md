# TradeBlocks 4.5.2

**ThetaData multi-day fetches skip weekends and market holidays, and an invalidated ThetaData session recovers once on its own. `filter_curve` gains an opt-in kept-share floor and fitting-iteration guidance on work refusals.**

4.5.2 fixes two ThetaData problems reported in #562; other providers and CSV import are unchanged. It also ships `filter_curve` changes for #586 items 1, 2 and 4.

## Behavior changes

### ThetaData fetches request trading days only

Previously, a multi-day intraday `fetch_bars` (for example `timespan: "1m"`) or per-ticker `fetch_quotes` sent one request per calendar day. ThetaData answers `NOT_FOUND` for a closed market, and that one failure aborted the whole range, so a range spanning a weekend wrote nothing.

Intraday `fetch_bars`, per-ticker and bulk `fetch_quotes`, and `fetch_chain` contract lists now skip weekends and XNYS market holidays without sending a request. Holidays come from the bundled XNYS calendar, which covers 2022–2030. Outside that range, weekends are skipped and every weekday is still requested, so a holiday before 2022 is reported like any other missing day. Daily bars are a single range request and are unchanged.

### Missing data on a trading day is reported, not hidden

A failure on a day the market was open is not treated as a holiday:

- **Intraday bars** fail the fetch with the date in the message (`ThetaData bars <date>: …`).
- **Per-ticker quotes** keep the successful dates and list the failed date in `requests[]` with an `error` outcome. The result status is `partial`, or `error` when every requested date failed.

### Session recovery

ThetaData allows one active session per account. Previously, when another terminal or client logged in with the same account, every later request failed with `16 UNAUTHENTICATED: Invalid session ID…` until the server was restarted.

TradeBlocks now logs in again once and retries the request with the new session. Requests that fail at the same time share that one login, so they do not invalidate each other. If the retry or the login fails, the error keeps the gRPC code and details and explains that another ThetaData terminal or client may be using the account: close it, then retry. A later request makes its own login attempt.

### `filter_curve` selection and admission clarity

- `filter_curve` accepts opt-in `minKeptFraction` (greater than zero, at most one). Best-threshold selection and every month-block permutation retain at least that share of the field-covered trade/day population after pre-filters and day aggregation. `keptShareFloor` reports the requested fraction, resolved minimum count, covered count and excluded candidate count. Descriptive threshold rows and sweet spots are unchanged; omission preserves existing selection behavior.
- If the floor leaves no eligible threshold, `bestThreshold` is null and requested permutation returns `no_eligible_threshold` without a p-value. Unavailable results continue to count conservatively in the Holm family.
- Work-limit refusals now include `maxIterationsThatFit`, the largest iteration count satisfying both existing admission limits for the unchanged request, or null with `iterationsFitReason` when even one iteration cannot fit. Estimator coefficients and limits are unchanged; day-unit admission continues to count entry days, not trades.
- Metadata wording change: `objectiveMetadata.maxDrawdownPercentBasis` and the MAR definition now explicitly identify capital at the start of the analyzed window, including prior P&L when `startDate` is set, rather than implying the block's initial capital. `startingCapitalSource` identifies daily-log or observed trade-funds evidence when available. No drawdown figure, capital resolution, CAGR or MAR calculation changed.

## Compatibility

- No setting or credential changes. The one new input, `filter_curve`'s `minKeptFraction`, is optional; omitting it preserves existing behavior.
- `MarketDataProvider` is unchanged. ThetaData's `fetchQuotes` now accepts the existing optional `onRequest` receipt observer; without an observer it throws on a failed date, naming the date.
- For consumers of `tradeblocks-mcp/test-exports`, `ThetaMddsClient` and the ThetaData endpoint functions keep their signatures.
