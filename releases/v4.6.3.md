# TradeBlocks 4.6.3

Security, market-data and replay fixes in the MCP server. Thanks to steffdax for the run_sql and enrichment reports.

## Changes

- run_sql now sandboxed inside DuckDB: reads limited to --data-root, no network or extension loading (reported by steffdax)
- import_market_csv re-imports recompute enrichment from the earliest changed date; enrich_market_data force_full works and accepts start_date/end_date (reported by steffdax)

## Tool output changes

- `import_market_csv` and `import_from_database`: the `enrichment` field changes from `{datesEnriched, from, to}` to `{recomputed[], lookbackFrom, changedDates, addedDates, removedDates, contextComputed}`.
- `enrich_market_data` adds `mode`, `recomputed` and `lookbackFrom`, and no longer returns `warning`.
- On the DuckDB backend, enrichment calls with date bounds now stay inside those bounds, matching the Parquet backend. Only an unbounded `enrich_market_data` call backfills older unenriched sessions.

## Replay changes

Three replay fixes merged since 4.6.2 also ship in this release. **The first changes replay results for Option Omega trades with a leg on the SPX monthly expiration: rerun those analyses.**

### Replay: Option Omega legs on the SPX monthly expiration replay SPXW

Option Omega trade logs name the underlying `SPX` for every SPX trade. On the
AM-settled monthly expiration (the third Friday, or the Thursday before it when
that Friday is an exchange holiday), `replay_trade` in tradelog mode replayed
those legs as the `SPX` monthly contract. Option Omega fills them from the
PM-settled `SPXW` contract instead: its fills equal the `SPXW` mid at the entry
minute, not the `SPX` monthly's. The replay priced a different contract from
the one in the trade. Its first bar already showed a profit or loss equal to the
gap between the two contracts' prices, and `replay_trade`'s `totalPnl` (measured
from the fills) disagreed with `decompose_greeks`' `totalPnlChange` (measured
from the first bar) by that amount.

A tradelog leg in Option Omega format now replays the `SPXW` contract on a
monthly expiration, and the output leg's `occTicker` shows `SPXW`. If no `SPXW`
quotes are cached, the replay returns an error naming that contract; it does
not fall back to the `SPX` monthly. This reverses, for Option Omega tradelog
legs only, the 4.5.1 statement that replay never substitutes `SPXW` on a
monthly expiration.

What changes: replay paths, totals and MFE/MAE for Option Omega trades with a
leg on a monthly expiration, and the results that `analyze_exit_triggers`,
`batch_exit_analysis`, `decompose_greeks` and `get_greeks_attribution` derive
from them. A trade that replayed only because `SPX` monthly quotes were cached
now needs `SPXW` quotes for that expiration. Rerun analyses of such trades.

What does not change: every other trade replays exactly as before. Explicit
`legs[]` (hypothetical mode) with `ticker: "SPX"` still replays the `SPX`
monthly, and `ticker: "SPXW"` still replays `SPXW`. Tradelog legs without
Option Omega per-leg prices are unchanged. Tool inputs and outputs keep the same
shape.

The docs now also state why `replay_trade` and `decompose_greeks` totals can
differ: by the first bar's P&L, the gap between the entry prices and the first
mids.

### Replay: January legs of December entries resolve to the next year

Option Omega trade logs name each leg's expiry without a year (`Jan 16`).
`replay_trade` in tradelog mode gave every leg the year of the trade's open date.
That put the January leg of a December entry, such as the back month of a
calendar or diagonal, in January of the same year, before the trade opened.
The replay found no quotes for that contract and refused the trade.
`analyze_exit_triggers`, `batch_exit_analysis`, `decompose_greeks` and
`get_greeks_attribution` reach the replay through the same path and refused or
skipped the same trades.

When an expiry would fall before the open date, it now moves to the next year.
A leg that expires on its open date keeps that year. Trades with no rollover
leg replay exactly as before. Explicit `legs[]` input (hypothetical mode)
carries full expiry dates and is unchanged. Tool inputs and outputs are the
same shape, and there is nothing to migrate. Rerun any analysis that refused
or skipped late-December entries.

### Replay: an unrecognized leg month is refused instead of read as January

In tradelog mode, `replay_trade` read the month of each Option Omega leg's
expiry (`Mar 13`) from a table that knew only `Jan`-style spellings. Any other
word, including an upper-case `MAR`, silently became January, and since the
year-rollover fix that January date could also move into the next year. The
replay then priced the wrong contract without saying so.

Months now match regardless of case, so `MAR 13` resolves to March. A word
that is not a month is refused with an error naming it, as an unparseable leg
already is. Option Omega writes `Jan`-style months, so existing trade logs
replay exactly as before. `analyze_exit_triggers`, `batch_exit_analysis`,
`decompose_greeks` and `get_greeks_attribution` reach the replay through the
same path. Tool inputs and outputs are the same shape, and there is nothing to
migrate.
